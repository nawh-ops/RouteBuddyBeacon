# QuodWords GB Territory Geometry — Phase 3 Visual Audit

## Status

The representative visual audit of the accepted GB coverage geometry has
been completed.

The audit used the tracked Phase 2 release artefact:

`tools/QuodWordsTerritoryGenerator/release/GB/gb-coverage-mask.geojson`

The geometry was converted from EPSG:3035 to WGS84 and inspected over
Google Earth imagery.

## Full-mask review

The complete GB land-and-marine mask was reviewed at national scale.

Accepted findings:

- the 25 nautical mile marine envelope is broadly smooth and consistent;
- no absurd long marine fingers were identified;
- no unexplained detached marine polygons were identified;
- Great Britain and Northern Ireland are included;
- Ireland, the Isle of Man, Jersey, Guernsey and France remain excluded;
- the overall mask is geographically plausible.

## Rockall

Rockall appears as a separate covered polygon west of Scotland.

This is expected under the accepted policy:

- Rockall remains covered as permanent GB land;
- Rockall does not independently generate a 25 nautical mile marine buffer.

The isolated Rockall geometry was visually inspected and accepted.

## Liverpool Bay cusp

A visually noticeable cusp south-east of the Isle of Man was investigated.

Approximate vertex position:

- longitude: `-3.761071645`
- latitude: `53.752933710`

Inspection of the neighbouring boundary vertices confirmed that the cusp
is a genuine meeting of marine-buffer boundary sections rather than a
single corrupt or accidental protruding vertex.

The feature is therefore accepted unchanged.

Cosmetic smoothing would alter the geometry generated by the documented
25 nautical mile coverage policy and is not appropriate.

## Representative island and border checks

The following areas were inspected over Google Earth imagery.

### Shetland, Foula and Fair Isle

Passed.

- the principal Shetland islands are covered;
- Foula and Fair Isle contribute to marine coverage;
- no detached marine blobs or unreasonable fingers were identified.

### Orkney

Passed.

- the principal and outlying islands are covered;
- coverage joins sensibly towards mainland Scotland and Fair Isle;
- no unreasonable artefacts were identified.

### Outer Hebrides and The Minch

Passed.

- Lewis, Harris, North Uist, South Uist, Benbecula and Barra are covered;
- Skye and the mainland west coast join into continuous coverage;
- The Minch is covered without an accidental internal gap;
- no unreasonable artefacts were identified.

### Isles of Scilly

Passed.

- the island group is covered;
- its marine envelope joins Cornwall cleanly;
- no detached artefact or unreasonable narrow bridge was identified.

### Northern Ireland and the Ireland border

Passed.

- Northern Ireland is included;
- the Republic of Ireland is excluded;
- Newry remains included;
- Letterkenny and Dundalk remain excluded;
- the land-border subtraction is geographically plausible;
- the Isle of Man remains excluded;
- no foreign-land slivers or unreasonable marine artefacts were identified.

## Conclusion

The accepted GB coverage mask has passed the representative Phase 3
visual audit.

No geometry alteration is required as a result of the visual inspection.

## Candidate island-area thresholds

The configuration retains the candidate island-area threshold values:

`0, 1, 5, 10, 25, 50 and 100 hectares`.

These values are audit-only experimental metadata used for geographic
comparison. They do not determine production buffer eligibility and are
not used by the production coverage-geometry generator.

They are intentionally retained unchanged because they form part of the
configuration whose checksum is recorded in the Phase 2 release
manifest. Removing or renaming them after acceptance would unnecessarily
alter that provenance.

No configuration or geometry change is required.
